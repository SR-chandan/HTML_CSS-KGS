Sericulture Process

Follow the complete journey from plant to cocoon

1

Grow Mulberry

Cultivate healthy mulberry plants as the primary food source for silkworms.

2

Rear Silkworms

Maintain clean rearing conditions and provide suitable mulberry leaves.

3

Cocoon Formation

Provide suitable mounting conditions for mature silkworms to spin cocoons.

4

Harvest Cocoons

Collect mature cocoons and prepare them for grading and further processing.

About SilkGrow

SilkGrow is a training platform designed to help farmers and beginners understand the complete process of sericulture.

Our goal is to provide simple, practical and easy-to-understand training about mulberry cultivation, silkworm rearing and cocoon production.

Farmers can learn the fundamentals, understand common risks and receive guidance to improve their farming practices.
